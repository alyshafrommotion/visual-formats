# Review — Classification Guide

## Definition

Customer reviews presented as the creative: one short quote and/or star rating, or several arranged together, product-forward, quick and skimmable. Whether it's a single pulled quote or a grid of review cards, it reads like real customer reviews brought to the front of the ad. It is a **quick hit**, not a story. One review or many, it's still Review.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Customer reviews are the hero** (a punchy line and/or a star rating), each attributed to a name/initial ("- Krista R."). This can be a single review or several.
2. **It's quick, not a narrative.** No arc, no start-to-finish transformation, no one telling their whole journey.
3. **Product stays in frame.** The review(s) sit on or beside the product, not a standalone talking head.

## Quick test

Is it customer reviews (one or several) carrying the ad, rather than a person telling their full story? If yes, Review. If someone walks through their whole before-to-after journey, it's `testimonial`.

## Review vs Testimonial (the key line)

- **Review** = quick review snippets, one or many, skim-in-two-seconds, product forward. Think "pulled quote card" or a grid of them.
- **Testimonial** = a person telling the **whole story**, almost always **video**: the struggle, the turning point, the result, in their own words over time.
- Rule of thumb: if you can skim it in a glance it's a Review (however many reviews are on it); if you have to watch someone narrate their journey it's a Testimonial.

## Commonly confused with

- **Testimonial:** full first-person story, usually video (see above).
- **Comment Screenshot:** a screenshotted social comment/DM or post, not a star-rating review card.
- Note: a grid or stack of multiple review cards is **still Review**, not a separate format.

## Signals

⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️, short quotes in quotation marks, first-name/initial attributions ("- Krista R."), product shot behind or beside the review(s), a single card or a grid of cards, "results may vary" footnote.

Examples in the files folder:

- O Positiv MENO "I lost 8lbs of menopause weight in 2026 because of THIS" - Krista R. (single quote over the product)
- Hers Ozempic (a grid of six 5-star review cards around the product)
