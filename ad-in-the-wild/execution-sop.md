# Ad in the Wild (OOH) Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

- **Reference examples:** They sit in this same folder, right next to this SOP.

**Whenever this SOP is triggered, open this folder and study the example first** — how the placement is framed, how real the scene reads, and how the inner creative is laid out on the surface.

## What this format is

The brand's ad staged as a physical out-of-home placement — a billboard, building wall, bus shelter, or transit poster — composited into a believable street or public scene, usually with a passerby for scale. It works because a big real-world placement reads as legitimacy and scale ("this brand is everywhere"), and the surprise of a familiar street holding the ad earns the stop.

Strongest for **brand-building, big statements/mission lines, hero-product moments, launch or "we've arrived" energy, and thumb-stopping pattern interrupts.**

The one property to protect: **it has to read like a real out-of-home placement in a real scene** — a fake billboard that looks fake kills the effect.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** and the **inner creative** (the poster/billboard artwork itself, which may follow another format), plus a believable scene direction. The angle + inner creative carry the message; this SOP governs the OOH staging.

## Anatomy — the layers

### 1 · The scene
- A real-world outdoor setting: street, plaza, building facade, transit stop. Natural light, real perspective, believable scale.

### 2 · The placement surface
- The OOH unit: billboard, wall-scape, bus shelter, poster. The creative sits on it with correct perspective and slight environmental wear/reflection so it reads real.

### 3 · The inner creative
- The actual ad: hook line, product, short benefit line, brand. Keep it billboard-simple — one big idea, legible from across a street.

### 4 · The human for scale (optional but strong)
- A pedestrian mid-stride, a cyclist, cars. Adds realism and shows the placement's size.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Sell the realism.** Correct perspective, lighting, and scale so it reads as a genuine placement. Any "obviously mocked-up" tell breaks it.
2. **Billboard-simple inner creative.** One big line, one product, minimal words — it has to work at a glance from across a street.
3. **Let the scene breathe.** Real environment around the placement; the surprise is seeing the ad in the wild.
4. **Add a human for scale** when it helps sell the size.
5. **Brand clearly on the placement.** OOH is a brand medium; make the logo legible.
6. **Specificity is credibility.** Real claims on the inner creative. No absolutes.
7. **One idea.** Don't cram a whole direct-response argument onto a billboard.
8. **Banned:** obviously fake/floating composites, warped perspective, unreadable tiny copy, "designed to," "seamless."

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Inner hook line | ≤8 words, billboard-legible |
| Inner support line | 1 short line |
| Words on the placement | keep minimal |
| Placements per ad | 1 hero (a montage of several is a variant) |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the inner creative, the scene direction.
2. Build/choose the inner creative billboard-simple (one big line + product + brand).
3. Choose a believable scene and OOH surface (billboard/wall/shelter/transit).
4. Composite with correct perspective, lighting, and scale; add environmental realism.
5. Add a human/vehicle for scale if it strengthens it.
6. Legibility check at thumb size; realism check (does it read as a genuine placement?).
7. Claims pass on the inner creative (validate numbers).
8. Deliver: scene/placement note, inner creative (hook, product, benefit, brand), realism/scale notes, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Reads as a real out-of-home placement in a real scene (perspective, light, scale)
- [ ] Inner creative is billboard-simple and legible at a glance
- [ ] Brand clearly on the placement
- [ ] Human/vehicle for scale where it helps
- [ ] Claims validated; no absolutes/banned phrases
- [ ] Any obviously-fake composite tells removed
