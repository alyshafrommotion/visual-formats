# Sign — Classification Guide

## Definition

A static or video where a **person holds a handmade sign** (cardboard/poster) with the core message handwritten on it, photographed in a real-world scene, usually with the product composited beside them.

## The things that must all be true

1. **A physical, held sign carries the headline.**
2. **It's shot as a real-world photo** — a person holding it in a place.
3. **The sign is handmade / handwritten**, not a designed graphic.

## Quick test

Is a person holding up a handmade sign that says the thing? If yes, Sign.

## Commonly confused with

- **Whiteboard:** a board propped/standing on a surface with marker; sign is *held* by a person.
- **Ad in the wild:** the brand's ad staged as a real OOH placement (billboard/transit), not a person's handmade sign.
- **UGC testimonial:** person talking to camera, no held sign.

## Signals

Person holding cardboard/poster, handwritten headline, street or store setting, product composited beside them.
