# Sign Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

They sit in this same folder, next to this SOP. **Open the folder and study the example first** — how the sign is held, how handmade it looks, and how the product is composited beside it.

## What this format is

A static (or video) where a person holds a handmade sign — cardboard/poster with the message handwritten on it — shot in a real-world scene, usually with the product beside them. It works because a real person holding a real sign reads as sincere, protest-like, or word-of-mouth, and the handmade look cuts through polished ads.

Strongest for **bold claims, social-proof statements, mission lines, and pattern-interrupt hooks.**

The one property to protect: **it must read as a real held sign in a real place** — not a designed graphic.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** and the one line for the sign. The angle carries the message; this SOP governs the staging.

## Anatomy

1. **The sign:** handmade cardboard/poster, message handwritten, held by a person.
2. **The scene:** a believable real-world setting (street, store, sidewalk).
3. **The product:** composited beside the person or in-shot.
4. **The support line + CTA:** short caption under the scene.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Handmade + held.** Real sign, real handwriting, held by a person in a real place.
2. **One bold line on the sign** — it has to land at a glance.
3. **Believable scene.** The realism sells the sincerity.
4. **Product legible** in-shot or composited cleanly.
5. **Short support line + one CTA.**
6. **Specific, real claims. No absolutes / banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Sign line | ≤8 words |
| Support caption | 1 short line |
| CTA | 1 |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the one sign line.
2. Write the sign line bold and short.
3. Stage a person holding the handmade sign in a believable scene.
4. Composite/position the product cleanly.
5. Add the support caption and one CTA.
6. Claims pass (real, specific; validate numbers).
7. Legibility + realism check at thumb size.
8. Deliver: sign line, scene note, product placement, caption, CTA, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Reads as a real handmade sign held by a person in a real scene
- [ ] One bold sign line, legible at a glance
- [ ] Product legible; short caption + one CTA
- [ ] Claims specific and validated; no absolutes/banned phrases
