# Press — Classification Guide

## Definition

An ad that borrows the visual language of press/editorial coverage: **real publication logos, an editorial-styled pull-quote, and/or "as seen in"/awards credibility**, arranged in a magazine-like layout.

## The two things that must both be true

1. **There's real, attributed third-party coverage on screen**: a named publication's logo, a quoted line with attribution, or a named award/rating, not a generic customer testimonial.
2. **The layout reads editorial**, serif type, clean grid, magazine-like spacing, not a standard promotional banner.

## Quick test

Ask: *is a named outside publication, quote, or award doing the persuading here, styled like a magazine or press clipping?* If yes, Press. If the proof is a customer comment/testimonial rather than press/media coverage, it's a different format.

## Commonly confused with

- **Instagram Comment:** also uses third-party proof, but it's an ordinary customer/social comment, not press/media coverage, and it's styled as app UI, not editorial layout.
- **Us Vs Them:** can also invoke external credibility, but its structure is a two-column comparison table, not a press pull-quote/logo bar.

## What to look for as supporting signals

- 3-6 recognizable publication logos or awards
- A pull-quote that is specific, not a vague adjective ("cut my CPA in half in 3 weeks" vs. "amazing product")
- Product shown inside the editorial frame, secondary to the proof
