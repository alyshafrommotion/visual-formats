# Behind The Scenes Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

They sit in this same folder, next to this SOP. **Open the folder and study the example first** — how the process footage, overlays, and voiceover build a credibility arc.

## What this format is

A video that takes the viewer inside how the product is made or developed — lab, manufacturing line, formulation, testing — usually with a founder or chemist voiceover on the process. It works because seeing the making earns trust that a claim alone can't.

Strongest for **premium/technical products, "why we're different" stories, ingredient/quality credibility, and skeptical audiences.**

The one property to protect: **it has to show real process** — staged, generic B-roll undercuts the whole point.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle**, real process footage (or a shot list), and a founder/chemist voice. The angle picks the credibility hook; this SOP governs the sequence.

## Anatomy (narrative arc)

1. **The hook question:** "What makes X different?" over a process shot.
2. **The reveal:** the actual making — lab, line, formulation, spec/blueprint overlays.
3. **The struggle/credibility beat:** "it took years with our chemists," archival R&D.
4. **The payoff:** product + brand line.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Show real process**, not generic B-roll. Lab, line, formulation, testing.
2. **Open on the difference question** to frame why the making matters.
3. **Let the founder/chemist voice carry credibility**, tied to what's on screen.
4. **Use spec/blueprint or archival overlays** to deepen the rigor.
5. **Keep it tight** and land on product + brand.
6. **Real, specific claims. No absolutes / banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Hook | first 2–3 sec |
| Total | ~15–30 sec |
| VO | conversational, tied to on-screen process |
| CTA/end | product + brand line |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the credibility story, available process footage.
2. Write the hook question.
3. Sequence the process reveal (making → struggle/credibility → payoff).
4. Script the founder/chemist VO to the visuals.
5. Add spec/blueprint or archival overlays where they add rigor.
6. Claims pass (process and claims real; validate numbers).
7. Deliver: hook, shot sequence, VO script, overlays, end card, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Shows real making/development process (not generic B-roll)
- [ ] Opens on the "why it's different" hook
- [ ] Founder/chemist VO tied to on-screen process
- [ ] Lands on product + brand; claims validated; no absolutes/banned phrases
