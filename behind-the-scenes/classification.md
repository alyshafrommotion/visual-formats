# Behind The Scenes — Classification Guide

## Definition

A video that takes the viewer **inside how the product is made or developed** — the lab, the manufacturing line, formulation, R&D, testing — usually with a founder or chemist voiceover on the process. It sells credibility through the making-of.

## The things that must all be true

1. **It shows the making / development process** (lab, line, formulation, testing).
2. **It's framed as an inside look** — how it's made, why it's different.
3. **Credibility / "you can trust this" is the payoff.**

## Quick test

Is the ad's power coming from seeing how it's actually made? If yes, Behind The Scenes.

## Commonly confused with

- **Founder:** founder-to-camera brand/origin story, not process footage.
- **Unboxing:** a customer opening the finished product.
- **UGC testimonial / demo:** using the product, not making it.

## Signals

Manufacturing line, beakers/lab glassware, blueprint or spec overlays, archival R&D clips, "what makes X different", "it took years with our chemists".

Example in this folder: Ilia "What makes skin tint different?" — lab, fill line, and chemist voiceover.
