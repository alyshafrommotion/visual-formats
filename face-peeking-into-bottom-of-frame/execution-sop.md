# Face Peeking Into Bottom Of Frame — Execution Guide

For "is this ad this format?" naming/classification questions, use `classification.md` in this same folder instead. This document is for building one.

## Why it works
- **It reads thrown-together on purpose.** The cut-off face, low angle, and blank background signal zero effort, which is disarming in a feed full of polished creative.
- **The face still grabs the eye.** A large human face low in the frame is an unusual composition, so it pulls attention even while feeling accidental, then hands the eye up to the text.
- **The plain background is free real estate for copy.** A featureless ceiling/wall behind the face means the text overlay sits clean with no design work.
- **Dead simple to produce.** Front camera, low angle, type the overlay. Ideal for organic volume.

## How to build one
Shoot a casual low-angle front-camera selfie so the face is cut off and sits in the bottom edge of the frame, against whatever plain surface is behind the person. Drop the text overlay in the empty space above the face. Keep it deliberately unstyled. Works with any copy or niche, since the shape is independent of the argument.
