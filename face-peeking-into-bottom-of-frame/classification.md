# Face Peeking Into Bottom Of Frame — Classification Guide

## Definition

A text-led static where the creator's **oversized, cut-off face pokes up into the bottom edge of the frame** from a low, off-kilter selfie angle, with a plain incidental background above carrying the text overlay.

## The two things that must both be true

1. **The face is cut off and positioned at the very bottom edge of the frame** (not centered, not a normal headshot), shot from a low angle.
2. **The background above the face is plain/incidental** (a ceiling, wall, car interior) and carries the text overlay, not a styled or busy scene.

## Quick test

Ask: *does this look like someone took a two-second selfie in a car or against a blank wall, with their face just barely poking into frame at the bottom, and text floating above them?* If yes, this format.

## Commonly confused with

- **Instagram Text Overlay:** also uses native-styled text over an image, but has no requirement for the specific low-angle cut-off-face composition. If the face isn't cut off at the bottom edge in this specific way, classify as `instagram-text-overlay` instead.
