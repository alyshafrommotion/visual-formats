# Us Vs Them — Classification Guide

## Definition

A **two-column comparison**: the brand on one side (green checks), an alternative (named competitor, category, or "the old way") on the other (red X's), across 4-7 rows of buyer-language criteria.

## The two things that must both be true

1. **There are two clearly labeled columns** (us vs. them/old way), not a single-sided list of benefits.
2. **Each row is a check/X (or equivalent binary) comparison** across a shared criterion, not annotated call-outs on one product.

## Quick test

Ask: *is this structured as a side-by-side table where the brand wins each row against a named alternative?* If yes, Us Vs Them.

## Commonly confused with

- **Feature Benefit Pointout:** also has multiple labeled points, but they're call-outs on a single product (feature → benefit), not a two-column brand-vs-alternative table.

## What to look for as supporting signals

- A framing headline that names the comparison's criteria
- 4-7 rows, strongest contrast first
- Either a named competitor (higher scrutiny, needs claim clearance) or "the old way"/category (safer default)
