# Unboxing Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

A video built around opening a packaged product on camera and revealing what's inside — the anticipation of the open, the contents laid out, the first look. It works because a reveal is a built-in scroll-stopper: people stay to see what's in the box, and seeing everything you get makes the purchase feel tangible and complete.

Strongest for **products with a satisfying physical package, "what you get" bundles/kits, gifting moments, and first-order/new-arrival launches.**

The one property to protect: **the reveal is the hook** — open on the anticipation or the moment of opening, never after the product is already out and explained.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** and the product's packaging plus everything that comes with it. The angle picks what to emphasize across the reveal (value, quality, ease, gift-worthiness); this SOP governs the reveal-led edit.

## Anatomy

1. **The reveal hook:** open on the sealed package or the first moment of opening — set up the anticipation in the first seconds.
2. **The open:** cut the tape / lift the lid / pull the wrap, close-up.
3. **The contents:** lift out and lay out what's inside; "here's everything that comes with it."
4. **The payoff:** first look at the product in use or styled, plus brand.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Open on the reveal.** Sealed box or the hands starting to open it — not the product already unpacked.
2. **Show the full contents.** Lay out everything included; make the "what you get" legible.
3. **Talk across it (usually).** Light voiceover or on-camera narration carries an unboxing; if you strip the talking and lead on amplified sound, you're building ASMR instead.
4. **Close-ups on packaging and first appearance.** The tactile detail of the package is part of the pitch.
5. **Land on first use or styled result + brand.**
6. **Honest claims. No absolutes / banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Reveal hook | first 1–3 sec |
| Total | ~15–40 sec |
| Voiceover | light to moderate |
| Captions | light |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the packaging, and everything included.
2. Pick the reveal hook (sealed package or first moment of opening) to open on.
3. Sequence the open → contents laid out → first look.
4. Add light voiceover/narration that carries the angle across the reveal.
5. Keep captions light; land on first use/styled result + brand.
6. Claims pass (any claim honest; validate numbers).
7. Deliver: reveal hook, shot sequence, VO/caption script, end card, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Opens on the reveal (sealed package or the moment of opening), not after unpacking
- [ ] Shows the full contents / "what you get"
- [ ] Reveal-led with talking (if it's sound-led with no talking, reclassify as ASMR)
- [ ] Close-ups on packaging and first appearance
- [ ] Lands on first use/result + brand; claims honest; no absolutes/banned phrases
