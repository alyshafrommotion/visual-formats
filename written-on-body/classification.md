# Written On Body — Classification Guide

## Definition

A static where the ad's message is **handwritten directly on a person's body** — stomach, arm, skin — turning the body into the copy surface. The pattern-interrupt is *where* the text sits.

## The things that must all be true

1. **The core message is written on skin / the body.**
2. **The body is the canvas for the copy**, not a caption or overlay.
3. **It's a real, UGC-style photo.**

## Quick test

Is the headline literally written on the person's body? If yes, Written On Body.

## Commonly confused with

- **Instagram-text-overlay:** digital text/stickers placed over a photo.
- **Sign:** message on a held sign, not on skin.
- **Testimonial:** a person talking, no body-written copy.

## Signals

Marker handwriting on a stomach or arm, mirror selfie or limb shot, product nearby.

Examples in the files folder:

- Grüns "Protein without gut support = WASTE" on the stomach
- Happy Mammoth "My 'impossible weight' is GONE." down the arm
