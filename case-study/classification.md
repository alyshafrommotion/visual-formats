# Case Study — Classification Guide

## Definition

A static framed as a **clinical / study proof**: a "clinically studied, visibly proven" headline plus several result rows, each a stat tied to a supporting image (before/after, product-in-use), usually grounded by a study-method footnote. It reads like a mini research writeup.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Explicitly framed as a study / clinical proof** ("clinically studied", "in a 12-week study").
2. **Multiple results**, each a stat paired with a supporting image or claim.
3. **A methodology / citation footnote** grounds the claims.

## Quick test

Does it present itself as evidence from a study, with more than one result and a method footnote? If yes, Case Study. If it's just one big number with no study scaffolding, it's `statistic`.

## Commonly confused with

- **Statistic:** big number(s) as the hero, no study framing or method.
- **Us vs Them:** brand-vs-alternative comparison.
- **Testimonial:** one person's story, not aggregated study results.

## Signals

"Clinically Studied, Visibly Proven"; stacked result panels each with an image and "X% saw … in 12 weeks†"; a small study-method footnote.
