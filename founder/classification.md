# Founder — Classification Guide

## Definition

The **person who built the product**, on camera, talking directly to the viewer, telling the origin/problem story or making a case only the founder can credibly make. First-person, low-polish, authority-driven.

## The two things that must both be true

1. **The speaker is the actual founder/builder of the product** (or is clearly presented as such), not a hired creator or spokesperson.
2. **The content is first-person origin/belief/mission material** ("I kept running into X," "here's why I built this," "I won't change this even though it costs more"), not a generic product pitch or feature walkthrough.

If the speaker is a paid creator with no founder framing, or the script is a generic testimonial rather than an origin/belief story, it's not Founder.

## Quick test

Ask: *is this specifically the founder telling their own story, in a way only they could credibly tell it?* If yes, Founder. If it's a creator or customer talking about the product without founder framing, it's a different format (likely Yapper if it's fast talking-head, or a testimonial-style format not yet in this library).

## Commonly confused with

- **Yapper:** also a talking-head monologue, but the speaker isn't the founder and the content is a fast persuasive rant/argument rather than an origin/belief story. If there's no founder identity and no origin-story spine, it's `yapper`.
- **Letter:** carries the same origin/belief/mission content, but delivered as written copy styled like a letter, not the founder speaking on camera. If it's read, not spoken, it's `letter`.

## What to look for as supporting signals

- Real, non-studio setting
- One concrete lived detail plus one admitted feeling
- Product enters late, as the resolution of the story, not the subject of the video
