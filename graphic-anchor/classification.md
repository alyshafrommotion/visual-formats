# Graphic Anchor — Classification Guide

## Definition

A **video** where a creator talks directly to camera and stays the anchor of the frame, while graphics (screenshots, images, UI cards, icons, product shots, b-roll) pop up **around** them, layered over that same shot, to clarify, emphasize, visualize, or extend what they're saying. The creator holds the frame the whole time; the graphics are a rotating support layer that changes with the script. They may or may not point at the graphics.

Graphic Anchor is almost always the **anchor (primary)** format and can pair with a **secondary** via the dual-format rule (e.g. graphic-anchor + listicle when the graphics enumerate a list).

## The things that must all be true

1. **It's a video.** Never a static.
2. **A creator talks directly to camera** and remains the full subject in a real setting.
3. **Graphics appear around/beside/above the creator, in-frame,** layered over the same shot (not a full-frame background behind them).
4. **Each graphic relates to the exact thing being said in that beat** — it clarifies, emphasizes, visualizes, or extends the line.
5. **The graphics rotate with the script:** new point, new graphic.

## The relationship (what the graphics do)

The graphics are there to add to the words:

- **Clarify / explain:** a screenshot or UI showing the thing being described (a ChatGPT-style chat box as they explain "asking in chat").
- **Emphasize:** an icon or big visual punctuating the point.
- **Visualize / metaphor:** a literal prop or image standing in for an abstract idea (an animated brain, a floppy disk, a plug for "connectors").
- **Extend / prove:** cards, diagrams, or b-roll carrying detail the VO doesn't have time for.

Pointing is optional. Placement and timing carry the link, not a gesture.

## Quick test

Mute it. Is it a real person talking to camera, with graphics popping up around them (not a full-frame screen behind them) that match what they're saying beat by beat? If yes, Graphic Anchor.

## Commonly confused with

- **Greenscreen:** the creator is composited in front of a full-frame background screen and the hook opens on that screen. In Graphic Anchor the shot is of the person in a real space and graphics float around them as accents; the frame is the person, not a screen.
- **Yapper:** a talking-head monologue with no on-screen graphics at all. Add graphics popping up around them and it becomes Graphic Anchor.
- **Feature-benefit-pointout:** a static with call-out lines on a product. Graphic Anchor is a video with the creator as the subject.
- **Founder / EGC:** those describe *who* is talking and the framing; Graphic Anchor describes the *graphics layer* around them. A founder or EGC ad with graphics popping up around them can dual-tag.

## Supporting signals (not required)

- Fast cuts as each graphic swaps with the point
- A mix of graphic types in one ad (real UI, literal visuals, metaphor props, b-roll)
- Graphics arranged around the creator's head/shoulders rather than replacing the shot
