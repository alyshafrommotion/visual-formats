# Whiteboard — Classification Guide

## Definition

A static or video built to look like a real **dry-erase whiteboard**: handwritten marker text and rough marker doodles on an actual whiteboard propped in a real setting (or a whiteboard graphic). Low-fi, homemade, argument-in-marker.

## The things that must all be true

1. **The message is handwritten in marker on a whiteboard surface.**
2. **The whiteboard aesthetic is the whole creative**, not a small element.
3. **It's usually a simple hand-drawn argument or comparison** (crossed-out vs checkmarked).

## Quick test

Does it look like someone made the point with a marker on a whiteboard? If yes, Whiteboard.

## Commonly confused with

- **Post-it:** handwriting on sticky notes, a different surface/aesthetic.
- **Notes-app / Letter:** typed, not hand-marker.
- **Sign:** a handmade sign *held by a person* in a scene; whiteboard is the board itself as the canvas.

## Signals

Dry-erase board, marker handwriting, crossed-out competitor doodle vs a checkmarked product, propped on a kitchen counter or desk.
