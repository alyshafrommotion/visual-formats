# Before And After — Classification Guide

## Definition

A static or video whose hero is a **single before→after transformation** of the same subject — same person's scalp, skin, hair, space — shown as a pair (before vs after N weeks/days). It usually carries a supporting stat, review, or study line, but the **transformation image is the centerpiece and the proof.**

## The things that must all be true

1. **A before and an after of the same subject**, shown as a pair (side-by-side or sequential).
2. **The transformation is the hero** — the visual change is what sells it.
3. **It's a genuine change-over-time comparison** (before → after the product).

## Quick test

Is the ad built around one before/after transformation of the same subject? If yes, Before And After.

## Commonly confused with

- **Case Study:** a structured, multi-result study writeup (several distinct stats, each with its own supporting image). Before And After centers **one transformation pair**; a supporting stat or footnote doesn't make it a case-study.
- **Statistic:** a big number as the hero, no transformation pair.
- **Us vs Them:** compares two *different* things (brand vs competitor/old way). Before And After compares the *same subject* over time. Some before/afters borrow us-vs-them framing ("toxic products" vs the brand) — if the two images are the same subject's change, it's Before And After (tag us-vs-them as a secondary if the competitor framing is strong).

## Signals

"Before" / "After" labels, "6 months" / "90 days," the same face/scalp/skin shown twice, a "results may vary" footnote, often a supporting stat or review.

Examples in this folder: Nutrafol Men (before / 6 months scalp), Moon Juice SuperHair (before / 90 days), Manual (before / after scalp), Primally Pure (acne before / after), Prose (before/after hair strands, video).
