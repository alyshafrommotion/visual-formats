# Statistic — Classification Guide

## Definition

A static whose hero is one or a few **big proof numbers** — result percentages, units sold, rankings — rendered large over a product or lifestyle shot. The number is the message.

## The things that must all be true

1. **One or a few large stats dominate the frame** (often as big percent type or ring charts).
2. **Each stat is a proof point** — a result, a volume, a ranking — not decoration.
3. **The number carries the ad**, not a scene, story, or comparison table.

## Quick test

Strip the copy away — is the thing you remember a big number? If yes, Statistic.

## Commonly confused with

- **Case Study:** a structured study writeup with a "clinically proven" frame, method footnote, and multiple result rows tied to images. Statistic is just the number(s) as hero, no study scaffolding.
- **Us vs Them:** a two-column brand-vs-alternative comparison.
- **Feature-benefit-pointout:** callouts on one hero product, not stats as the hero.

## Signals

"94% saw…", "More than 2 million bottles sold", "#1 selling", ring/donut charts, oversized percentage type over a model or product.
