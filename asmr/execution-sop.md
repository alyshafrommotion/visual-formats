# ASMR Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

They sit in this same folder, next to this SOP. **Open the folder and study the example first** — the close-ups, the amplified sounds, and how little voiceover there is.

## What this format is

A video built around amplified, satisfying real-world sounds of using, installing, or unpacking the product — tactile clicks, ratchets, peels, pours — ASMR-style, with sound as the hook and minimal or no voiceover. It works because crisp, satisfying sound is a strong scroll-stopper and makes the product feel tactile and high-quality.

Strongest for **satisfying-to-use products, install/assembly/prep processes, unboxing-adjacent moments, and sound-on feeds.**

The one property to protect: **the sound is the star** — clean, amplified, close-mic'd tactile audio.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** and the product moments with the most satisfying sounds. The angle picks which moments to feature; this SOP governs the sound-led edit.

## Anatomy

1. **The sound hook:** open on the most satisfying sound in the first seconds.
2. **The process:** close-up, step-by-step footage (install, prep, unpack) with amplified audio per action.
3. **Minimal text:** light captions only; let sound lead.
4. **The payoff:** finished result + brand.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Lead with sound.** Open on the crispest, most satisfying audio moment.
2. **Close-up, tactile footage** for every step; amplify each action's sound.
3. **Minimal/no voiceover.** If muted, it should still look satisfying, but sound is the point.
4. **Clean, quiet setting** so the product sounds carry.
5. **Light captions only.**
6. **Honest claims. No absolutes / banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Sound hook | first 1–2 sec |
| Total | ~15–30 sec |
| Voiceover | none or minimal |
| Captions | light only |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the most satisfying product moments.
2. Pick the sound hook (the crispest moment) to open.
3. Sequence close-up process steps, amplifying each action's audio.
4. Keep captions light; land on finished result + brand.
5. Sound design pass (clean, close-mic'd, amplified tactile audio).
6. Claims pass (any claim honest; validate numbers).
7. Deliver: sound hook, shot/sound sequence, captions, end card, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Opens on the most satisfying sound
- [ ] Close-up tactile footage with amplified audio per step
- [ ] Minimal/no voiceover; clean setting; light captions
- [ ] Lands on result + brand; claims honest; no absolutes/banned phrases
