# Flatlay — Classification Guide

## Definition

A static shot from directly **overhead** of the product (or products + props) **arranged flat on a clean surface** — a styled "flat lay." The arrangement itself is the creative, often showing a full range/variety.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Top-down / overhead angle** looking straight down.
2. **Product(s) laid out flat on a clean surface**, deliberately styled.
3. **The layout / variety is the hero** (the range, the palette, the spread).

## Quick test

Is it a top-down styled arrangement of the product laid flat? If yes, Flatlay.

## Commonly confused with

- **Feature-benefit-pointout:** callouts/arrows pointing at one hero product's features. Flatlay has no pointing; it's a styled overhead spread.
- **Product/hero shot:** a single product beauty shot (often angled), not a flat overhead arrangement.
- **Listicle / benefit list:** a list of points; flatlay is a visual arrangement, usually with minimal copy.

## Signals

Overhead angle, fan/grid arrangement, a color or variant range shown together, clean neutral background, minimal copy ("Get one in every color").

Example in this folder: J.Crew shorts fanned out in every color, shot from directly above.
